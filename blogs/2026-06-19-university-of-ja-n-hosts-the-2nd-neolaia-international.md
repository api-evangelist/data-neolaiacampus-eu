The University of Jaén welcomes more than 150 researchers, educators, and institutional leaders from 22 countries for the 2nd NEOLAiA International Symposium on Language Policy. The event explores English-Medium Instruction (EMI), plurilingualism, AI in multilingual education, and strategies for shaping the future of global higher education.
