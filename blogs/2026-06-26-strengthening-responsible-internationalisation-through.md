Staff from seven NEOLAiA universities gathered at Örebro University for the Work Package 4 (Enhanced Mobility) Staff Training Network on Responsible Internationalisation. The programme encouraged the exchange of good practices, strengthened professional collaboration, and explored inclusive and sustainable approaches to internationalisation in higher education.
